# Contributing to AGI

Thank you for your interest in AGI. We are building a native, 100% on-premise AI ecosystem designed to guarantee digital sovereignty and absolute privacy. By contributing, you are helping democratize AI technology and breaking the dependency on extractive cloud-based services.

## Our Vision
We believe that AI should be a tool for the user, not a product of large corporations. Our core values are:
* **Zero Telemetry:** No data ever leaves the local machine.
* **Hardware Optimized:** We focus on running quantized models on accessible hardware.
* **Native Performance:** Our core is built in C# for low-latency, asynchronous interactions.

## How You Can Help
We welcome contributions in several areas:
1. **Core Development:** Improving the C# engine or optimizing the interaction between the AI and the graphic engine.
2. **3D & UI:** Enhancing the interactive tutor using the Godot Engine.
3. **Model Optimization:** Testing and refining quantized LLMs for different hardware profiles.
4. **Documentation:** Helping us make our installation and deployment protocols more accessible.

## Development Process
We utilize the **SCRUM framework** for development to ensure iterative improvements and stability.
1. **Fork the repository** and create your branch from `main`.
2. **Check the Project Board** to see our current Sprints and priorities.
3. **Submit a Pull Request (PR):** Describe your changes clearly and ensure your code follows .NET 8.0 standards.

Building a truly sovereign AI requires collaborative effort. We appreciate your support in making this technology accessible to everyone.
